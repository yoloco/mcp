# Example requests for an influencer marketing campaign

Copy a request into Claude, ChatGPT or Codex after connecting the Yoloco MCP server. Replace the
details with your own. The assistant quotes the price of anything paid before it runs.

## 1. Brief and plan

* "Help me write a brief for a skincare launch in Germany. Ask me one question at a time."
* "Turn the brief into a search plan for Instagram and TikTok with a budget of 300 coins."

## 2. Find creators

* "Find 30 Instagram beauty creators in Berlin with 50k to 200k followers, mostly women aged 18 to 34."
* "Show TikTok creators in Spain about fitness with high audience quality. Free preview first."
* "Find YouTube tech channels in the US with over 100k subscribers and a mostly male audience."
* "Find Telegram channels similar to @example_channel and add them to my list Tech TG."
* "Find Instagram creators with an audience like @creator_a and @creator_b. Free results first."

## 3. Vet the shortlist

* "Which of these creators has my team already opened reports for? Those are free to reopen."
* "Open the audience report for @creator and tell me if the followers look real."
* "Compare the shortlist by engagement rate, audience country and suspicious followers."
* "Give me the contacts of the three finalists for outreach."

## 4. Build the media plan

* "Create a media plan Spring launch and add everyone from the list Finalists."
* "Set the price of @creator to 1200 EUR and show the plan totals and the planned CPM."
* "Export the media plan to Excel."

## 5. Check audience overlap

* "How much do the audiences of these five Instagram creators overlap? Tell me the price first."
* "Which two creators share the most followers, and whom would you drop?"
* "Keep the four creators that reach the most unique people within 5000 EUR."

## 6. Audience segments

* "Build a segment of Instagram creators whose audience is into running and marathons."
* "Add the top 50 creators of that segment to a new list."

## 7. Competitor research

* "Which creators did Brand X sponsor on YouTube in the last 6 months?"
* "Show the best performing sponsored posts of that report and add the channels to a list."

## 8. Track the campaign

* "Track these published posts as the campaign October launch, with the prices from the media plan: <links>."
* "Compare the October campaign with its media plan: who delivered the views we paid for?"
* "How is the October campaign doing? Which posts are below the median engagement?"
* "Give me the morning brief of all running campaigns."
